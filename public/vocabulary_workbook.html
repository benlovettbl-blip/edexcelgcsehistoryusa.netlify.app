Key Topic 1 Glossary & Match-up: Civil Rights, 1954–60

Core Term Definitions
Segregation: The legal and social separation of Black and White people in public life.
Disenfranchisement: The system of blocking citizens from using their constitutional right to vote.
Litigation: Taking legal cases to federal courts (e.g. NAACP targeting segregation).
Boycott: Peaceful commercial protest involving refusal to buy/use segregated services.
Precedent: A legal decision serving as a binding guide for future rulings (e.g. Brown v. Topeka).

Key Topic 2 Glossary & Match-up: Civil Rights, 1960–75

Core Term Definitions
Militancy: Using direct, confrontational, or armed methods to demand immediate reform.
Legislation: Statutory acts passed by Congress to outlaw segregation (e.g. Civil Rights Act).
Integration: The blending of segregated races in schools, transport, or public areas.
Separatism: The goal of separating Black communities to form independent social institutions.
De facto: Segregation existing by custom and economic reality, rather than by law.

Key Topic 3 Glossary & Match-up: US & Vietnam, 1954–68

Core Term Definitions
Containment: Foreign policy designed to block the geographical spread of communism.
Escalation: Broadening military commitments (e.g. deploying thousands of combat troops).
Defoliation: Destroying jungle canopy using chemicals (e.g. Agent Orange) to expose cover.
Guerrilla: Hit-and-run tactics, ambushes, and tunnel networks used by the Vietcong.
Stalemate: A deadlock where neither side can make significant military progress.

Key Topic 4 Glossary & Match-up: Vietnam End, 1969–75

Core Term Definitions
Vietnamisation: Nixon's policy of withdrawing US troops and building up South Vietnamese forces.
Conscription (Draft): Compulsory state military draft to select soldiers for Vietnam combat.
Silent Majority: Nixon's term for the moderate public who did not participate in war protests.
Moratorium: Large coordinated public anti-war protests taking place in major cities.
Credibility Gap: Public skepticism resulting from contradictions in government military reports.
