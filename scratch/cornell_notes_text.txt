What were the roles of the NAACP and CORE? KT1.2: Developments in Education Brown v. Topeka (1954): What were the causes and the significance of the ruling? Little Rock High School (1957): Key events and the role of President Eisenhower. KT1.3: Montgomery Bus Boycott (1955-60) Causes: Why did the boycott start? (Rosa Parks). Success: Why was it successful? Consider the Supreme Court and MLK's leadership. Impact: Significance of the SCLC and the 1957 Civil Rights Act. KT1.4: Opposition to Civil Rights The KKK: Methods and the murder of Emmett Till (1955). Political opposition: White Citizens' Councils and the 'Dixiecrats'. KT2: Civil Rights Progress, 1960–75 Cues & Questions (Left) Notes (Right - Student to Fill In) KT2.1: Progress 1960-62 Greensboro Sit-ins (1960): Significance of student-led protest. Freedom Riders (1961): Key features and the Anniston bomb. James Meredith (1962): How did he challenge the University of Mississippi? KT2.2: Peaceful Protests 1963-65 Birmingham (1963) & March on Washington: Impact of 'I Have a Dream'. Freedom Summer (1964): Mississippi murders and voter registration. Selma (1965): Events and the 1965 Voting Rights Act. KT2.3: Malcolm X and Black Power Malcolm X: Nation of Islam beliefs vs. his later views. Black Power: Stokely Carmichael and the 1968 Mexico Olympics. Black Panther Movement: Methods and achievements. KT2.4: Civil Rights 1965-75 Riots of 1965-67: Causes and the findings of the Kerner Report. MLK in the North: Successes and failures. Assassination impact. KT3: US Involvement in Vietnam, 1954–68 Cues & Questions (Left) Notes (Right - Student to Fill In) KT3.1: Reasons for Involvement (1954-63) Dien Bien Phu: End of French rule and the division of Vietnam. Eisenhower: Domino Theory and the weaknesses of the Diem gov. Kennedy: Overthrow of Diem and the Strategic Hamlet Program. KT3.2: Escalation under Johnson Gulf of Tonkin Incident (1964): How did this lead to full war? KT3.3: Nature of Conflict (1964-68) Vietcong: Guerilla tactics (tunnels, booby traps, Ho Chi Minh trail). USA: Search & Destroy, Operation Rolling Thunder, Chemical weapons. Tet Offensive (1968): Key features and why it was a turning point. KT4: Ending the Conflict in Vietnam, 1969–75 Cues & Questions (Left) Notes (Right - Student to Fill In) KT4.1: Opposition to the War Why did opposition grow? (TV coverage, draft system, students). My Lai Massacre (1968) and Kent State (1970): Public reaction. KT4.2: Support for the War Fear of Communism and the 'Silent Majority'. KT4.3: The Peace Process Nixon: Vietnamisation, the Nixon Doctrine, and Kissinger's role. Attacks on Cambodia (1970) and Laos (1971). Paris Peace Agreement (1973) and the cost of the war. KT4.4: Failure in Vietnam Strengths of NV: Russian/Chinese support and tactics. US Weaknesses: Morale, failed tactics, and domestic opposition.
